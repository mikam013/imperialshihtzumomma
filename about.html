About the breeder

Hi, I'm the Shih Tzu Momma

I'm an AKC Shih Tzu breeder. After many years on California's Central Coast, I moved with my family to Yadkinville, North Carolina, and the nursery came with me.

I breed purebred Shih Tzus in small standard and Imperial sizes, in lots of colors. I love their babydoll faces and how affectionate they are.

Every puppy is raised in our home with hands-on care. They're socialized with kids and other dogs, so they're ready to join your family.

How we breed

  • Every adult is health tested before breeding
  • Parents are AKC registered and Embark DNA tested
  • Each girl has only a few carefully planned litters
  • Retired dogs go to loving, hand-picked homes

Our promise to you

  • A written contract with a 1-year health guarantee
  • Help and advice for your dog's whole life
  • If you can ever no longer keep your dog, we will take them back or help rehome them
