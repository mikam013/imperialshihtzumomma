Getting your puppy

How it works

Before you apply, make sure a Shih Tzu fits your life and get any permission you need from a landlord or partner.

  1. Apply

    Fill out the application. Tell us what you'd like: color, gender and size.

  2. Pay the deposit

    Once approved, a $200 deposit holds your spot. It goes toward the puppy's price and is non-refundable.

  3. Join the waitlist

    You hear about new litters before the public. We keep first pick of each litter.

  4. Choose your puppy

    Prices are set at about 6 weeks, based on size, color and quality. Puppies start at $3,500.

  5. Go home

    Pay the balance before pickup. Puppies go home at about 10 weeks and at least 2 lbs.

Every puppy comes with

  • Starter kit: toys, collar, bowls and some food
  • Folder with vet records
  • Contract with a 1-year health guarantee
  • A head start on potty training
  • Support from us for life

Getting your puppy home

  • Pick up in person in North Carolina
  • Courier delivery
  • Out of state: we can refer a flight nanny who carries your puppy on the plane

Can't keep your dog someday? We will always take them back or help rehome them.

Questions

What is an Imperial Shih Tzu?

A smaller Shih Tzu than the AKC standard. The AKC does not list Imperial as a separate breed, but the Alianz Italian Kennel Club does recognize it. We breed both Imperial and small standard sizes.

How much does a puppy cost?

Puppies start at $3,500. The final price is set at about 6 weeks, based on size, color and quality.

Is the waitlist first come, first served?

Not always. If you want a specific color or gender, you may wait for the right puppy while others go ahead of you.

Do puppies come with breeding rights?

Puppies are sold with a spay/neuter agreement. A few are offered to small breeding programs, and those are noted on the listing.

I sent my application. When will I hear back?

Usually within a few days. Check your spam folder too. If you haven't heard back, email imperialshihtzumomma@gmail.com.
